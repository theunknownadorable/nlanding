About Me

I'm an Assistant Professor in Computer Science & Engineering, and an AI/ML researcher who still writes more Python than prose. My work sits where theory meets a classroom full of students who will absolutely ask but why does it work?

Post-graduate in Computer Science from TKM College of Engineering, now teaching at TKM Institute of Technology. I research machine unlearning and SISA — making models forget specific data without retraining from scratch — and I build classroom robots that make attendance feel like an experiment.
